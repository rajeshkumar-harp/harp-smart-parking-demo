# HARP Smart Parking — Demo

A premium resident app for **HARP's automated robotic parking**. This is a hands‑on
**demo build** that runs entirely on sample data — no account, no setup, works fully
**offline**.

---

## ⬇️ Download

**→ [Download the Android app](../../releases/latest)** &nbsp;·&nbsp; `HARP-Smart-Parking-Demo-v1.1.apk` &nbsp;·&nbsp; ~62 MB &nbsp;·&nbsp; Android 7.0+

## Install

1. Open the downloaded **`.apk`** on your Android phone.
2. If prompted, allow **"Install unknown apps"** for your browser / file manager.
3. If Google Play Protect shows a warning, choose **"Install anyway"** — this is
   expected for a pre‑release demo build.
4. Open **HARP Smart Parking**.

## Sign in

Enter **any mobile number** → **Send code** → **any 6‑digit code**. You're in.
*(It's a demo — no real account or OTP needed.)*

## Try these (5 minutes)

1. **Your car, live** — the home screen shows your parked vehicle, its slot, and how long it's been parked.
2. **Park My Car** — drive‑in → the **safety‑confirmation gate** → live parking → your **digital ticket** (QR). Try Save / Share.
3. **Retrieve** — bring your car to the exit bay; watch the live **ETA, queue position, and progress ring**.
4. **EV Charging** — tap the **battery ring** on a charging car → the live charging detail (state of charge, kWh, cost).
5. **Guests** — add a guest → generate a **QR access pass**.
6. **Two buildings** — tap the building name under the greeting to switch between **Skyline Residences** and **Marina Heights**.
7. **Payments & Leasing** — invoices and EV charges, plus the **Parking Leasing Marketplace**.
8. **Theme** — *Account* → switch **Dark / Light**.

## Good to know

- Everything runs **offline on sample data** — tap anything; nothing is real or permanent.
- This is an early build — we'd love your feedback on the experience and the design.
